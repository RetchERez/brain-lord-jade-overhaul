[b]Brain Lord: Jade Overhaul v1.0 (SNES)[/b]

Brain Lord with four additional fairy roles while keeping the original adventure intact. All 13 original individual fairies remain, with Rapid, Ward, Frost and Poison bringing the total to 17. The original Crimson-only opening and Life's Silver Plaque exchange are preserved. There is no added shopkeeper.

[b]Features[/b]
- All 13 original individual fairies preserved, including their original counterparts
- Rapid, Ward, Frost and Poison added as four optional dungeon discoveries
- Original shops, Jade sources, prices, sphere drops and story gates retained
- 50 EXP for each equipped eligible fairy from each collected sphere
- Distinct new fairy sprites, projectiles and inventory Jade icons
- Specific Jade names before the naming keyboard
- Native Poison damage flashes and hit sounds, without added stun or knockback
- Original two-fairy limit and ROM size retained

[b]The new fairies[/b]
Rapid / Zeph - Weak purple shots that become faster through training
Ward / Aegis - One contact block when charged, then a recharge
Frost / Rime - Weak icy shots and a movement slow that does not affect bosses
Poison / Briar - One-target damage over time, no impact damage or stacking, and no boss damage

[b]Installation[/b]
- Use a clean, unmodified, headerless Brain Lord USA ROM
- Original ROM size: 1,572,864 bytes
- Original ROM CRC32: AC443D87
- Apply the BPS patch once to that clean ROM and save under a separate filename
- Start a new game for all four new treasure rewards
- Keep saves from earlier 12-fairy builds and emulator save states separate

The ZIP contains only the BPS patch and readme. Version 1.0 uses v10 in the filename. The readme lists all 17 Jade locations.

[b]Verification[/b]
26 native verification groups, 66 unit tests and 9 model regressions passed. All 136 pairs were compared mathematically and all 272 slot orders were exercised in native combat, recall and room reload checks. No full campaign playthrough was performed.
