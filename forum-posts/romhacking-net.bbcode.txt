Brain Lord: Jade Overhaul adds four new fairy roles while preserving all 13 original individual fairies, for a total of 17. The original adventure and two-fairy equipment limit remain intact.

[b]Four new companions[/b]

[b]Rapid / Zeph[/b] fires small purple shots. Its attacks start weak and become faster through training.

[b]Ward / Aegis[/b] blocks one eligible enemy contact hit when charged, then recharges. It starts uncharged and does not attack.

[b]Frost / Rime[/b] fires icy shots that slow one ordinary enemy's movement. A cyan aura marks the effect. Bosses and boss parts are not slowed.

[b]Poison / Briar[/b] uses thorn shots to apply damage over time to one ordinary enemy. Damage ticks use the game's normal hit feedback and sounds, without added stun or knockback. Poison does not stack or affect bosses.

[b]Features[/b]

- Four optional dungeon rewards contain the new Fairy Jades
- Each new fairy has its own equipped sprite, effect and inventory Jade icon
- Each collected blue sphere gives 50 EXP to each equipped eligible fairy
- Naming prompts identify the Jade whose fairy is being named
- Original fairy stats, Jade sources, merchants, prices and sphere drops are preserved
- The opening still gives Crimson alone, and Life keeps its Silver Plaque exchange
- No added shopkeeper or ROM expansion

[b]Installation[/b]

Apply the included BPS patch to a clean, headerless Brain Lord USA ROM. The original ROM must be 1,572,864 bytes with CRC32 AC443D87. Start a new game to make all four new treasures available.

The readme includes installation details, save information and all 17 Jade locations. Keep saves from earlier 12-fairy builds and emulator save states separate.
